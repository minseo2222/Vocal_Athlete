## 6. Topics Still Needing More Research (Gap List)

Priority follow-ups (ordered):

1. **Trans / gender-affirming voice training** — primary refs to add: Davies & Goldberg (2006/2015 *J Voice* + WPATH SOC v8 voice section); Mills & Stoneham (2017) *The Voice Book for Trans and Non-Binary People*; Hancock et al. (2014–2024) — entirely missing from corpus.
2. **Pediatric / adolescent voice and changing voice** — Cooksey, Gackle, Sweet refs needed.
3. **Choral pedagogy specifics** — Smith & Sataloff *Choral Pedagogy* and Jordan's *Evoking Sound*; current corpus underweights choral training.
4. **Korean-language primary-source corpus** — most research relies on English secondaries; need Korean academic journals (말소리와 음성과학 / 음성과학 / 한국음악교육학회) directly.
5. **Long-term outcome studies of method brands** — most CVT/Estill/SLS evidence is short-term; longitudinal cohort RCTs are scarce.
6. **K-pop industry training documentation** — most sources are journalistic; need primary corporate training manuals (likely proprietary).
7. **Multilingual diction beyond top-4** — Russian, Czech, Spanish (different varieties), Mandarin/Cantonese, Japanese — lightly covered.
8. **Vocal injury epidemiology** — Sataloff & Hawkshaw cohort data, prevalence in CCM vs classical, recovery trajectories.
9. **AI / real-time feedback technology evaluation** — moving target; needs continuous review.
10. **Ethnomusicological voice traditions** — beyond pansori: Bulgarian women's chest, Mongolian khoomei, Inuit katajjaq, Sardinian cantu a tenore — each has distinct technique that may inform pedagogy.

## 7. Methodology Note

Phase 1 used file structure (header-level grep) and selected full reads to build the topic map without redundantly consuming context on 25k-token files. Phase 2 employed parallel WebSearch batches across high-priority topics, prioritizing PubMed/PMC, ScienceDirect/Journal of Voice, ASHA pubs, ResearchGate, and primary institutional sites. Authority filtering: rejected blog-only posts unless tied to a credentialed pedagogue; accepted dissertation-level work where peer-reviewed equivalents were thin (e.g., for Lee 2017 Korean IPA dissertation). Where a single source covered multiple topics (e.g., Titze 2006), it is listed once with cross-references implicit in the topic clustering.

> **End of compilation.** All inline URLs are verified-format references gathered from web search responses. For any topic where the user wants a deeper dive, see the Gap List in §6 — those are the highest-leverage follow-ups.
