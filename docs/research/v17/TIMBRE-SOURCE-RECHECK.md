# TIMBRE SOURCE RECHECK — v17

v17 adds six additional spot checks to the timbre/source bundle. These sources are used as **P-class curriculum-structure anchors** only: they show that serious vocal training programs include technique, artistry, repertoire, coaching, performance projects, and professional preparation. They do **not** prove that a mobile app alone can produce professional-level singing outcomes.

## Curriculum implication

The checked sources reinforce the v17 stance:

- keep **Universal Vocal Core** as the common engine rather than early genre branching;
- keep **Repertoire Application** as vocal training transfer, not song production;
- require **performance/review artifacts** before advanced claims;
- keep advanced genre tracks unreleased until safety, coaching, and content QA are stronger.

## Evidence boundary

These six checks are official program or institutional pages. They are useful for curriculum scope and vocabulary, but they are not dose-response studies, mobile-app efficacy trials, or safety-limit studies. Concrete take limits, lesson counts, and weekly caps remain **D-class product hypotheses** until tested.
