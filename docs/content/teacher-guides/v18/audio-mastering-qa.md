# Teacher Guide Audio Mastering QA

## File checks

- lossless master retained;
- release derivative format documented;
- no clipping or unintended distortion;
- leading/trailing silence is short but not abrupt;
- speech and sung examples are intelligible on a phone speaker at a conservative device volume;
- low and middle variants have comparable perceived level;
- file path, duration, codec, sample rate, channels, and SHA-256 recorded.

## Playback checks

Test at minimum on representative Android devices and one iOS device:

- phone speaker;
- wired or USB headset where supported;
- Bluetooth route;
- low and medium device-volume settings;
- interruption and resume behavior.

A peak or loudness target is an engineering consistency check, not proof of a universally safe listening level. The app must retain low-volume guidance and user-controlled playback.
