# Universal Core Cycle 1 Day 6 — Hum-to-Vowel Transfer

## Learning intent

Reproduce the Day 37 coordination after a delay and carry it into a very short two- or three-note contour while preserving ease.

## Spoken introduction

> 먼저 예시 없이 기억에서 한 번 해봅니다. 편한 한 음의 ‘음–마’를 만든 뒤, 작은 두세 음 모양으로 옮깁니다. 음역을 넓히는 날이 아닙니다.

## Sequence

1. unaided `/m/ → /ma/` recall;
2. optional low or middle guide after the first attempt;
3. small contour on hum;
4. the same contour on `/u/` or `/ma/`;
5. user selects one self-check: ease, stable loudness, or smooth opening.

## Transfer rule

Only one condition changes at a time. If pitch contour is introduced, vowel, tempo, and loudness remain stable.

## No-voice recovery alternative

Listen once, tap the contour timing, and mark where the vowel opens. No phonation or whispering.

## Limits

- maximum 2 attempts;
- no maximum-range test;
- no belt, strong twang, pressed attack, or prolonged sustain;
- if the learner reports tiredness, use one attempt or no-voice mode.
