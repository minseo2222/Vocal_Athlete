# FIRST TIMBRE PILOT PROTOCOL — v17

This pilot checks whether the first timbre vertical slice is understandable, safe-feeling, and motivating. It does not validate vocal improvement.

## Participants

- 5–10 adult beginner or low-intermediate users.
- Exclude users with current pain, active hoarseness, recent vocal injury, or medical voice concerns.
- Participants should be told that this is app usability feedback, not diagnosis or voice assessment.

## Tested flow

1. Beginner Day 37 `TONE-02` Hum-to-Vowel.
2. Beginner Day 38 `TONE-03` Vowel Color Taste.
3. Universal Core Cycle 1 Day 6 `TONE-02`.
4. Tone tag selection.
5. A/B recording panel.
6. Tone Profile screen.
7. v17 tag edit and palette exclusion.
8. Tired/hoarse recovery path.

## Key questions

- Do users understand that tone tags are self-labels, not AI judgments?
- Do users understand `bright`, `warm`, `clean`, and `speech-like` without over-pushing?
- Does the two-take cap feel sufficient or frustrating?
- Do users keep the same recording condition?
- Can users find and understand the Tone Profile?
- Can users correct or withdraw a tag without feeling they are deleting their recording?
- Does `hoarse` state actually stop voiced tasks?
- Does the app accidentally encourage whispering or forced brightness?

## Stop criteria

Stop the pilot task immediately if the participant reports:

- pain;
- raw/strained throat sensation;
- hoarseness worsening;
- dizziness;
- pressure to be louder or higher;
- confusion that makes them repeatedly retry.

## Success threshold for v18 planning

Proceed to broader content expansion only if at least 80% of participants:

- understand tone tags as self-reports;
- finish Day 37/38 without pushing volume or pitch;
- can explain the difference between deleting a take and excluding it from the palette;
- correctly choose recovery/no-voice mode when hoarse;
- do not interpret Tone Profile as a fixed voice type.

## Data to collect

- screen recording or observer notes;
- selected tags;
- number of takes;
- whether same-condition checkbox was meaningful;
- qualitative confusion points;
- no raw audio export unless explicitly consented.
