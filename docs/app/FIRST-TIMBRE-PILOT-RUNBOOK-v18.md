# FIRST TIMBRE PILOT RUNBOOK — v18

This runbook operationalizes the v17 protocol. It is a usability and safety-comprehension pilot, not a vocal-efficacy study or medical assessment.

## Participants

- 5–10 adults who identify as beginner or low-intermediate singers;
- do not run voiced tasks when a participant reports current pain, worsening hoarseness, recent vocal injury, or a medical voice concern;
- explain that tone tags are editable self-descriptions, not AI classifications.

## Session order

1. consent and device-volume check;
2. Beginner Day 37;
3. edit one tone tag and add a second tag;
4. exclude and restore one take in Tone Profile;
5. Beginner Day 38;
6. Universal Core Day 6 delayed recall;
7. switch to tired/hoarse and verify no-voice behavior;
8. short interview and metadata-only export review.

## Observer tasks

Record whether the participant:

- understands low/middle examples are references, not required pitches;
- keeps pitch and volume stable while changing one variable;
- understands multiple tags can coexist;
- understands exclusion is not audio deletion;
- finds the two-attempt cap acceptable;
- stops phonation when hoarse mode appears;
- interprets the palette as a diary, not a fixed voice type.

## Stop criteria

Stop the voiced task immediately for pain, worsening hoarseness, raw/strained sensation, dizziness, repeated pressure to go louder/higher, or persistent instruction confusion.

## Decision rule

Do not broaden timbre content unless critical safety-comprehension failures are resolved. The previous 80% comprehension target remains a product gate, not statistical proof of efficacy.
